# PDP Copy — Business Model & Go-to-Market

## 1. Value Proposition

**For Store Owners**:
- Write 500+ product descriptions in hours instead of weeks
- Consistent brand voice across all products
- SEO-optimized copy (improves Google ranking + CTR)
- No copywriter hiring costs ($3k-10k/month savings)

**For Marketing Agencies**:
- 10x faster client deliverables
- Upsell service: "Professional Description Rewrite"
- Recurring revenue through client subscriptions
- Competitive differentiator vs. other agencies

---

## 2. Revenue Model

### Primary: SaaS Subscription (80% of revenue)

**Pricing Tiers**:
- **Starter**: $99/month (100 products/month)
- **Professional**: $249/month (500 products/month)
- **Enterprise**: $599/month (unlimited)

**Expansion Revenue**:
- Overage charges: $5/product (Starter tier)
- Custom integrations: +$200-500 one-time
- Consulting/voice training sessions: $99/hour

### Secondary: Agency Partnerships (15% of revenue)
- **Revenue Share**: Agencies sell PDP Copy to clients
  - PDP Copy earns 30% of subscription revenue
  - Agency keeps 70%
  - Example: Agency sells to 20 stores @ $249/month = $2.98k MRR → PDP Copy gets $894/month per partner

### Tertiary: Marketplace Extensions (5% of revenue)
- **Future**: Sell pre-built "voice profiles" (e.g., "Luxury Fashion", "Tech Products")
  - One-time purchase: $9-29 per voice profile
  - Also available free with Professional tier

## 4. Unit Economics

### Cost Per Rewrite
```
AI API Cost (OpenAI):
  Input tokens: 500 @ $0.03/1K = $0.015
  Output tokens: 200 @ $0.06/1K = $0.012
  Total per rewrite: ~$0.03

Infrastructure (amortized):
  n8n: $500/month ÷ 10,000 rewrites = $0.05
  Database: $200/month ÷ 10,000 rewrites = $0.02
  
Total Cost Per Rewrite: ~$0.10
```

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Assumptions**:
- Marketing spend: $2,000/month (Facebook/Google ads)
- Conversion rate: 5% (software targeting e-commerce)
- Avg deals: 10 new customers/month

**CAC**: $2,000 ÷ 10 = $200 per customer

**LTV (Lifetime Value)**:
- Avg subscription: $250/month (blended)
- Avg churn rate: 5% (20-month LTV)
- LTV: $250 × 20 = $5,000

**LTV:CAC Ratio**: 5000:200 = 25:1 (excellent)

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Validation & Beta (Months 1-3)
**Goal**: Acquire 25-30 beta customers, validate PMF

**Actions**:
1. **Launch private beta**: 20 invite-only Shopify stores
   - Offer free/discounted access in exchange for feedback
   - Weekly check-ins; collect case studies

2. **Content marketing**: 
   - Launch blog: "How to Write Product Descriptions That Sell"
   - Create YouTube tutorial: "Shopify + PDP Copy setup" (5 min)
   - Post on Shopify Community forums

3. **Direct outreach**:
   - Email 500 Shopify store owners (AngelList, ProductHunt, Shopify forums)
   - Offer 2-week free trial + $20 credit

4. **Strategic partners**:
   - Reach out to Shopify app reviews (AppAdvice, ShopReadyStudio)
   - Partner with Shopify expert consultants (referral commission)

**Target metrics**:
- 25-30 beta signups
- 20+ rewrite case studies
- Product-market fit confirmed

---

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Months 4-6)
**Goal**: Launch on Shopify App Store; reach 100 paying customers

**Actions**:
1. **Shopify App Store listing**:
   - Submit app for approval
   - Professional screenshots, demo video
   - Pricing display, free trial setup

2. **Organic growth**:
   - SEO targeting: "Shopify product description AI", "Shopify copywriting tool"
   - Blog posts optimized for long-tail keywords

3. **Paid acquisition**:
   - Google Ads ($500/month): Target high-intent keywords
   - Facebook/Instagram: Retarget website visitors
   - Budget: $2,000/month marketing

4. **PR/media**:
   - Pitch to tech blogs (Product Hunt, Launch newsletter)
   - Guest post: "The Future of AI Copywriting" (on Medium, HubSpot Blog)

5. **Community**:
   - Join Shopify Store Owners Facebook groups
   - Provide value (free description tips) before promoting

**Target metrics**:
- 100 paying customers (mix of Starter/Professional)
- 50k monthly impressions
- 5% conversion rate on free trial → paid
- $8-10k MRR

---

### Phase 3: Growth & Scale (Months 7-12)
**Goal**: Reach 200+ customers; $40k+ MRR

**Actions**:
1. **Partner program**:
   - Shopify experts / agencies: 30% revenue share
   - Target 20-30 partner agencies
   - Provide: white-label option, training, co-marketing

2. **Product expansion**:
   - Launch WooCommerce integration
   - A/B testing feature (Phase 2)
   - Multi-language support

3. **Scaled marketing**:
   - Increase ad spend to $5k/month
   - Launch affiliate program (Shopify influencers)
   - Host webinar: "E-Commerce Copywriting Masterclass" (lead gen)

4. **Customer retention**:
   - Implement onboarding email sequences
   - Monthly product updates/newsletters
   - Customer success manager (part-time)

**Target metrics**:
- 200+ paying customers
- $40k MRR
- 15% churn rate (good for SaaS)
- 90% NPS

## 10. Risk & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| AI output quality too low | Low adoption | Medium | Voice training; human review mode; iterate prompts |
| Shopify API changes | Technical issues | Low | Monitor API docs; maintain backward compatibility |
| Customer churn (high seasonality) | Revenue loss | Medium | Annual billing discount; multi-year contracts |
| AI cost exceeds revenue | Profitability loss | Low | Switch to cheaper models (Claude); optimize prompts |
| Competition from Shopify | Market disruption | Low | Build integrations they can't; first-mover advantage |
